How the agent's options develop

Green circle: initiation state. Orange diamond: target / termination state. Blue arrows: actions supported by the option policy; child-option calls point to the child's target. Arrow labels show selection probabilities (demonstration fractions in older snapshots).

These are closed-loop cloned policies, not fixed action sequences. Options also stop at unseen states or their step limit. Counts describe demonstrations used when the option was created. Policies are frozen in this implementation; manager values and selection counts can change.
